Company Remote Work Policy

1. Purpose
This policy explains the company's general expectations for employees who work remotely or use a hybrid work arrangement. The goal is to support productivity, collaboration, security, and a healthy work environment.

2. Eligibility
Remote or hybrid work may be available to employees whose roles can be performed effectively outside the office. Eligibility is determined by the employee's role, team requirements, business needs, and manager approval.

3. Working Hours
Employees are expected to follow their assigned working schedule and remain available during agreed collaboration hours. Employees should inform their manager in advance when they will be unavailable during required working hours.

4. Communication
Employees working remotely must maintain reliable communication with their team. Company-approved communication tools should be used for business discussions. Important decisions and project updates should be documented in appropriate company systems.

5. Data Security
Employees must protect company information when working remotely. Confidential information should not be shared with unauthorized people. Company devices should be protected with strong passwords and should not be left unattended in public places.

6. Company Equipment
Company equipment provided for remote work should be used primarily for authorized business purposes. Employees are responsible for taking reasonable care of company laptops, phones, and other equipment.

7. Workspace
Employees should maintain a workspace that allows them to perform their duties safely and effectively. Employees should take reasonable steps to protect confidential information from being viewed by people who are not authorized to access it.

8. Meetings
Employees are expected to attend required meetings on time. When participating in video meetings, employees should follow team expectations regarding cameras, microphones, and professional conduct.

9. Performance
Remote employees are expected to meet the same performance and quality standards that apply to employees working from the office. Managers may evaluate performance based on agreed goals, responsibilities, deliverables, and collaboration.

10. Leave and Absence
Remote employees must follow the company's normal procedures for requesting leave and reporting absences. Working remotely does not replace the requirement to obtain approval for planned leave.

11. Security Incidents
Employees must promptly report lost company devices, suspected unauthorized access, accidental disclosure of confidential information, or other security incidents to the appropriate company contact.

12. Policy Violations
Failure to follow this policy may result in corrective action in accordance with company rules and applicable law.

13. Policy Updates
The company may update this policy when business requirements, technology, security practices, or applicable laws change.
